# Privacy Policy

Backup App works only with the Google account of its owner, who authorized it.

It uploads backup files to that account's Google Drive and reads only files it needs for the backup. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it does not send any data to third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Last updated: 25 October 2026.
